How it works

• Each tile you place costs 1 brick; bulldozing is free.
• Bricks come from levelling up — every 100 XP earns 3.
• Zones only activate next to a road.
• Residential grows near jobs; industry pollutes; parks and schools clean the air and lift happiness.
• Higher city levels unlock new tools and speed up growth.
